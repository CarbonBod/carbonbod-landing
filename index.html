Even our sensors are open source.

"Verify, don't trust" cannot stop at the registry. Every MRV sensor design — schematics, firmware, bill of materials — is published openly. Anyone can build them; anyone can inspect them; local firms can manufacture them. Only devices passing the public conformance suite feed credit-issuing data. Open door. Guarded data.
